How often does each bot win?

Two bots of one type sit against two of another, and every deal is played twice with seats swapped, so card luck cancels out. A bot wins a game when its party scores points. Partners are dealt by the Kreuz-Damen, so a bot often plays with the other type — that keeps every rate fairly close to 50 %.
